How this was built

Sources and fine print

November 2026 races

Florida House figures come from the Florida Division of Elections (through Oct. 29). Congress figures come from the FEC (through Oct. 14). School Board totals were reported by Jacksonville Today. Platforms are mostly from the News4JAX voter guide.

Where the 2027 numbers come from

Campaign treasurer reports filed with the Duval County Supervisor of Elections. Most figures are from the Q3 2026 report (through Sept. 30, 2026). Each card links to that candidate's filings.

What the numbers mean

Raised is money contributions only (not donated goods or services). Cash left is raised minus spent, our math, not a reported balance. Self-funded is money the candidate put in or loaned to their own campaign.

Spending and donor categories

We sorted each itemized line into categories by what it says (for example "print," "consulting," "processing fees"). They're close, not official. Check the filing for exact lines.

Platforms

Taken from the candidate's own website, their statement on file with the Supervisor of Elections, or news coverage, and summarized in neutral words. If we couldn't find one, we say so. We don't guess.

The field will change

Nobody is officially on the ballot until qualifying, noon Jan. 11 to noon Jan. 15, 2027. Candidates can still enter or drop out. Withdrawn candidates aren't shown.

Photos

Official City Council portraits and photos candidates filed with the Supervisor of Elections. Candidates without one on file show initials. Candidates can send a photo and we'll add it.
